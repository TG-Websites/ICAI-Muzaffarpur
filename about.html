Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI

Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI

The Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI is a statutory body established under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1984 for the regulation and development of the profession of Chartered Accountants in India.

Since its inception in 2024, the branch has been committed to empowering future professionals through academic programs, skill-based workshops, and collaborative efforts with various institutions and local bodies.

Inaugurated by Hon'ble President Shri CA Ranjeet Kumar Agarwal on 16th August 2024, the branch serves 200+ members and over 2400+ students in the Muzaffarpur region.

With a strong focus on academic excellence and professional ethics, the branch regularly conducts seminars, interactive sessions, mentorship programs, and networking meets to foster continuous learning and development.
