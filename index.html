About Muzaffarpur Branch

Learn more about the values, mission, and services of the Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI.

The Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI plays a vital role in shaping the future of the accounting profession in the region. We are committed to providing quality professional education, guidance, and support to our members and students. We aim to foster an environment of learning, ethical practice, and professional development. Our branch also strives to enhance the knowledge base of the accounting community through various initiatives, events, and programs.

Mission & Values

  • Provide exceptional education and training for members and students.
  • Promote ethical practices and professional excellence in all fields of accounting.
  • Encourage networking, collaboration, and skill development among professionals.
  • Support the growth of the accounting profession in Muzaffarpur and beyond.

It is a statutory body established under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (Act No.XXXVIII of 1949) for the regulation of the profession of Chartered Accountants in India. Muzaffarpur Branch of CIRC of ICAI Empowering Accountants Since 2024 with Dynamic Academic Programs and Workshops.

A Message From Chairman

Dear Members and Aspiring Cas With great humility and profound pride, I pen this message as the First Elected Chairman of the Newly Constituted (Notified on 22nd January 2024 & Inaugurated on 16th August 2024) Muzaffarpur District Branch of CIRC of ICAI.

This isn’t just a role—it is a dream realized. A dream that began years ago when I first stepped into the world of accountancy as a student, filled with ambition, guided by mentors, and driven by the singular goal of adding “CA” before my name. Today, having walked that journey, I stand before you not only as a proud member of ICAI but as a torchbearer of this newly formed branch—entrusted with the responsibility to lead, to inspire, and to serve.

Our branch is more than just an institution—it is a symbol of growth, opportunity, and unity for the CA fraternity in this region. We now have a dedicated platform to nurture talent, promote professional excellence, and build a stronger community.

From “DRISHTI” as Secretary 2024-25:

  • D - Digitalization
  • R - Research
  • I - Integrity
  • S - Skills
  • H - Handholding
  • T - Transparency
  • I - Independence

To “VISHWASNIYA” as Chairman 2025-26:

  • V - Vision for Viksit Bharat
  • I - Integrity & Ethics
  • S - Sustainability & Social Impact
  • H - Holistic Professional and Leadership Development
  • W - Wellness & Work-life Balance
  • A - Accelerating Digital Transformation
  • S - Stakeholder Engagement
  • N - Nation-first Approach
  • I - Innovation & Entrepreneurship
  • Y - Youth and Women Led Development

Our Vision Forward:
As Chairman, my focus will be on building:

  • A vibrant hub for members with seminars, workshops & networking.
  • A launchpad for students with mentorship and career support.
  • A pillar of credibility aligned with ICAI’s values.
  • An inclusive, ethical, and innovative community.

This branch belongs to all of us. I invite every member and student to participate, share ideas, and shape its future. Together, let’s build not just a branch, but a legacy.

With warm regards,
